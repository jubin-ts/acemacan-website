Safety Systems

Fire & Safety Solutions

ACEMACAN supplies certified fire alarm systems, fire protection equipment, and comprehensive safety solutions for commercial buildings, industrial facilities, and residential projects. Our products comply with UAE Civil Defence regulations and international safety standards including NFPA and BS EN.

We partner with leading fire safety manufacturers to provide complete solutions — from conventional and addressable fire alarm panels to fire suppression systems, emergency lighting, and smoke detection. Our team provides end-to-end support from product selection to installation coordination.

Protection

HSE Equipment & PPE

Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) compliance is critical for any industrial or construction site. ACEMACAN supplies a comprehensive range of personal protective equipment (PPE) and HSE solutions sourced from certified manufacturers, meeting international standards including EN, ANSI, and ISO.

Our HSE product range covers everything from basic personal protective equipment like safety helmets and goggles to specialized items like fall protection harnesses, gas detectors, and complete PPE kits. We provide bulk supply for large-scale projects with competitive pricing and reliable delivery timelines.
